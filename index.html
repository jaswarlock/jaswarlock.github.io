Developing coupled electro-chemo-mechanical models simulating solid lithium evolution inside solid-state electrolytes to prevent dendrite growth and void-induced failure.

  • ⚡ Coupled Nernst–Einstein & Stress Tensors: Formulated a 2D computational model incorporating multi-directional mechanical stresses (including shear), Nernst–Einstein ion diffusion, advection, and electric-potential gradients across charge/discharge cycles.

  • ⚡ Vacancy Limit Verification: Demonstrated the absence of excess vacancy formation under both charge and discharge regimes, validating the model against theoretical transport limits.
